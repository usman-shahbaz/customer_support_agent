# Orders

## Order Processing

Orders are processed within 24 hours after payment confirmation.

Orders placed after 5 PM are processed on the next business day.

Customers receive an email confirmation immediately after placing an order.

---

## Order Tracking

Once an order is shipped, a tracking number is emailed to the customer.

Customers can track their shipment using the courier's tracking website.

---

## Order Modification

Orders can be modified before shipment.

Customers should contact customer support as soon as possible.

---

## Order Cancellation

Orders may be cancelled before shipment.

Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled.
